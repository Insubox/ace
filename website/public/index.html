01 · KNOWLEDGE

Keep the evidence. Reuse compatible lessons.

Native history retains winner and loser source, reports and generator faults. Reuse requires compatible contracts and fresh tests; a retrieved lesson alone does not qualify new code.

■ proven□ rejected■ open
02 · OBJECTIVE

Define what a better result means.

Freeze the baseline, dependencies and independent suite. Compare passing cases and resource observations, and keep previously passing cases passing.

03 · TRIAL

Bounded proposals. Complete projects.

Hello World, TicTacToe and Calculator campaigns compile and test complete projects in isolated containers. Invalid generator output is recorded separately from incorrect code.

04 · CHAMPION

A verified improvement, ready for review.

A native branch advances only for measured improvement without regressions. Production release additionally requires independent approval, exact-source evaluation and verified deployment.
